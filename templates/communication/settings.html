How to Configure Gmail App Password

Gmail requires a dedicated 16-character App Password instead of your regular Google account password.

  1. Open your Google Account Security Settings.
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled.
  3. Search for "App Passwords" in the security search bar.
  4. Enter an app name (e.g. Sky Vision ERP) and generate a 16-character token.
  5. Copy the 16 characters into your .env file under EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD=xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx (spaces are ignored).
Documentation See SETUP_GMAIL.md in project root for full troubleshooting details.
